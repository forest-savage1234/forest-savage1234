# Forest Savage

Independent researcher and inventor. I publish open-source tools, experiments, and technical work.

## Selected work

### [Decision Review Ledger](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/dfrws-lead-review)

A Python prototype that tracks the evidence behind a decision and requests human review when mapped observations change. My work includes the standalone ledger, controlled evaluation, and replay tools. Research prototype evaluated on fictional cases.

[Source and setup](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/dfrws-lead-review#run-the-engine) · [Evaluation and limits](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/dfrws-lead-review#original-controlled-ledger-evaluation)

### [Casefile Provenance Demo](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/casefile-provenance-demo)

An interactive demonstration of the ledger using a fictional case. I built the browser presentation and integration, with controls that distinguish a changed assessment, an editorial change, and a missing dependency.

[Open demo](https://forest-savage1234.github.io/casefile-provenance-demo/) · [Source and verification](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/casefile-provenance-demo)

### [CAC Ontology contribution record](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/CAC-Ontology-v4-Contribution-Record)

An engineering record of my foundational v4 proposal: semantic modeling, dependency analysis, validation, and reproducibility evidence. Unofficial contributor proposal; full-import conformance remains unresolved.

[Proposal and evidence](https://github.com/forest-savage1234/CAC-Ontology-v4-Contribution-Record) · [Upstream discussion](https://github.com/Project-VIC-International/CAC-Ontology/pull/49)
